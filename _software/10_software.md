LegalIA is a monorepo of Python packages for the analysis of legal texts in the Mexican context. Its first target is the *Diario Oficial de la Federación* (DOF), Mexico's official gazette, with more than 1.2 million legal provisions published without interruption since 1917. The packages download the gazette and the Supreme Court's federal legislation, convert legal provisions to Markdown, segment them into retrieval units and provide embeddings of every federal law, regulation and treaty.
